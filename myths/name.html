Myth 2 · The whole story

That he changed his name three times to hide who he is

Mixture: he was born James Donald Bowman, but he did not officially change his name three times, or when he married.

Last updated November 19, 2026, 6:50 PM PT. This page keeps the full record behind the short card on the Myths vs. facts page: what was claimed, what the fact-checks found, a timeline, and the primary evidence.

What they said

A 2024 social-media meme said Vance was “born as James Donald Bowman,” then “changed middle name to David,” “changed name again to Jimmy Hamel,” and “changed name to JD Vance 10 years ago when he married his wife.” One version was titled “WHO ARE YOU?” and asked whether he was “hiding” something.

Who spread it: widely shared image macros on X and similar posts in the summer of 2024, after Vance became the Republican vice-presidential nominee. Snopes examined a post that had more than 51,000 views when it was reviewed, and readers sent in close variants of the same meme.

What is true

He was born James Donald Bowman, in Middletown, Ohio, on Aug. 2, 1984, named in part for his biological father. That much of the meme is true. The rest mixes up the legal record and invents a motive.

There were two official name changes, not three, and neither was a disguise. The first came when his stepfather, Robert Hamel, adopted him. His mother changed his name to James David Hamel — keeping the initials “J.D.,” which were already his nickname. Middletown City Schools, his hometown district, says that change happened in 1990, the year he started kindergarten, and that James David Hamel is the name he used throughout school. He graduated from Middletown High School in 2003. The Associated Press, which reviewed documentation and Ohio vital records, reported that the only birth certificate on file for him at the state’s vital-statistics office reads James David Hamel, because the adoption removed the Bowman name from the public record.

The meme’s “Jimmy Hamel” is not a name in that record. The second official change, to Vance — his grandmother Bonnie Blanton Vance’s family name — became legal in April 2013, as he was about to graduate from Yale Law School, a year before he married. His spokesperson, Taylor Van Kirk, told the AP that it “didn’t happen on his wedding day in 2014, as the book implies.” Dropping the periods in “J.D.” later on was a stylistic preference, not a third legal change. His legal name is James David Vance.

Snopes rated the meme a mixture: true that he was born James Donald Bowman; false that he officially changed his name three times, or that the last change came “when he married.” The AP’s reporting, built on state records and documents from his school, the Marine Corps, Ohio State, and Yale, matches that timeline.
