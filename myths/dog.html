Myth 3 · The whole story

That he admitted his dog Atlas was a campaign “rent-a-dog”

False: the clip was cut to hide a joke about an online rumor.

Last updated November 23, 2026, 9:45 AM PT. This page keeps the full record behind the short card on the Myths vs. facts page: what was claimed, what the fact-checks found, a timeline, and the primary evidence.

What they said

In mid-September 2024, short clips of Vance’s interview with Tucker Carlson were posted as if Vance had admitted that his German shepherd, Atlas, was “a rent-a-dog that was given to me by the campaign to make me seem like I’m a dog fan.”

Who spread it: videos and captions on TikTok, Threads, and X around Sept. 21, 2024, cut from a Sept. 18 interview on Carlson’s Rumble show. One Threads caption said, “The campaign got JD a dog to make him appear more human.” Another said, “JD Vance admits he has a ‘rent-a-dog.’”

What is true

The clips were cut in a way that hid the setup. In the full interview, Vance was mocking an online rumor, not confessing to it. He introduced the dog by saying, “this is Atlas, which I found out on the internet a few weeks ago that he’s actually a rent-a-dog that was given to me by the campaign to make me seem like I’m a dog fan.” He then said, “Of course, we got him when he was an 8-week-old puppy,” and that it was “shocking” anyone would think otherwise.

PolitiFact rated the claim False and wrote that “the video was deceptively edited.” FactCheck.org reached the same conclusion: the posts “misrepresent” the comments. A Vance spokesman told PolitiFact the family got Atlas as an 8-week-old puppy in January 2024 — before Vance became the vice-presidential nominee.
