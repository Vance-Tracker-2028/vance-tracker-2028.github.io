Methodology

This is an unofficial supporter site that tracks public polling and large-account posts and outlet coverage concerning Vice President JD Vance. It was independently created by supporters of JD Vance. It is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by JD Vance, his office, any campaign, committee, or the White House, and it is not a scientific sample of all public opinion. No funds are solicited or accepted.

Items are included only when a source page or post was opened for this update. Every item has a source link. Follower counts and engagement figures are as returned by the source; they are left blank when unknown. Summaries are factual descriptions, not endorsements.

X posts are generally from accounts with 50,000 or more followers, or from a notable official or outlet. Authenticity ratings (Likely organic, Mixed, or Signs of inauthentic amplification) are heuristics based on visible signals such as account age, follower and following mix, default avatars, digit handles, posting volume, duplicate wording, and engagement that is out of line with the account. They are not proof, and no specific person is labeled a bot.

Sentiment totals use likes, reposts, and quotes on X posts only. Replies are omitted because they mix support and pushback. Vice President Vance’s own originating post is excluded from those totals. Donald Trump Jr.’s endorsement is shown both included and excluded because it accounts for most supportive reach in this set.

Favorability averages cite the published RealClearPolitics average where a page was opened, and a simple unweighted mean of listed polls. The 2028 Republican nomination average is an unweighted mean of each candidate’s share in the national polls opened for this update. Those polls use different universes and are not strictly comparable. A RealClearPolitics 2028 nomination page was blocked (403) and was not used.

Dates and the “last updated” stamp are in America/Los_Angeles (Pacific Time). Search window for this edition: about November 3–18, 2026.
